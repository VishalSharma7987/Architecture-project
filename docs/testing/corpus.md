# The real-drawing corpus

**Status:** specified, not yet collected. This is the actual deliverable of Stage 1.

---

## Why the fixtures we have prove almost nothing

`samples/` holds six renderings of one plan, generated by `samples/gen-blueprint.mjs`. Three of them — `colour`, `noisy`, `thin` — were added in the **same uncommitted change set** as the detector code they exercise:

| Fixture added | Detector capability added alongside it |
|---|---|
| `blueprint-colour.svg` | `paperContrastMasks` — Chebyshev distance from the dominant colour |
| `blueprint-thin.svg` | `MIN_RASTER_DIMENSION` upscaling with nearest-neighbour |
| `blueprint-noisy.svg` | `scoreSegments` candidate selection |

Testing a detector against drawings authored to suit it is circular. A green suite here means the detector still does what it did yesterday — which is worth having, and is what `src/blueprint/detectWalls.golden.test.ts` is for — but it says nothing about whether it works.

**The golden harness proved this on its first run.** It found two defects that all six generated fixtures had passed through:

1. `paperContrastMasks` selected **the paper itself** (87–95% of the image as "ink"), because the dominant colour is quantised to 5 bits per channel and lands at 252 on a white sheet, making every pure-white pixel "lighter than the paper". On a plain sheet the resulting blob was rejected downstream for being too thick, so the fault stayed invisible. On a gridded sheet the grid lines sliced that white field into 75 long thin strips with excellent aspect ratios, and `scoreSegments` — which maximises total detected length — preferred 77,592 px of imaginary wall to 6,300 px of real one. **Two of the three PNG fixtures detected zero real walls.**
2. `mergeWallFaces` fused a partition with an adjacent **door swing arc**, because it checked overlap but never that the two bands were the same *length*. The partition came back 0.64 m thick and displaced 0.26 m — five times its real size, and straight into the 3D model and the cost sheet.

Both were in unshipped work. Both would have shipped.

## What the golden harness asserts, and what it deliberately does not

`samples/README.md` says *"Import any of the above, export the result, diff against this."* A whole-document diff fails on every fixture, for two reasons that are both correct behaviour:

| Not compared | Why |
|---|---|
| **Openings** | `blueprint-expected.json` lists 11. `detectWallSegments` deliberately does not detect openings — a door reads as a gap that `mergeCollinear` heals back into one wall, and openings come from the vision model. Diffing them would test the LLM. |
| **Exact coordinates** | The detector reports the median centre of an ink band; a renderer strokes a 16 px wall and antialiases its edges. Demanding equality asserts a rounding mode. Tolerance is 0.02 m (2 px at 100 px/m). |
| **Exact endpoints** | `snapJunctions` pulls an end onto a crossing wall's centreline, so a detected wall legitimately stops half a thickness short of where the drawing painted it. Length is compared by **overlap**. |
| **Document version / settings** | The fixture is `version: 1`, which now migrates to v2 and gains a `blueprint` field it cannot know about. |

What **is** asserted, per fixture:

1. every ground-truth wall has a match — same axis, centreline within 0.02 m, ≥60% length overlap;
2. each thickness is right to within 0.04 m;
3. no more than 2× the ground-truth wall count is returned (the annotation filters still work);
4. a 12 m wall carrying two doors comes back as **one** wall ≥90% of its true length, not three fragments;
5. **all three presentations yield the same wall count** — polarity, annotation and colour are presentation, and none should change what imports.

## The corpus that has to replace it

**Target: ≥50 real architect drawings.** Not generated, not redrawn. Sourced from practices, municipal sanction submissions, real-estate listings, and scans of printed sets.

### Taxonomy

Every drawing is tagged on **all** applicable axes. Pass rate is tracked **per tag**, never as a single number — a corpus-wide "84%" hides that hatched walls are at 0%.

**A. Wall rendering** — the axis the detector is most sensitive to
| Tag | What it is |
|---|---|
| `wall-solid` | Filled black band. The easy case. |
| `wall-outlined` | Two parallel lines, hollow between. `mergeWallFaces` exists for this. |
| `wall-hatched` | Two lines with 45° hatch or a poché tint between. |
| `wall-tinted` | Two lines with a flat fill between. |
| `wall-double` | Cavity wall drawn as four lines. |
| `wall-hairline` | Under ~3 px at source resolution. `MIN_RASTER_DIMENSION` exists for this. |

**B. Sheet and polarity**
`sheet-white` · `sheet-blueprint` (white on blue) · `sheet-scan` (grey cast, skew, JPEG artefacts) · `sheet-photo` (phone, perspective, uneven lighting, shadow) · `sheet-coloured` (rooms flooded with floor tints) · `sheet-greyscale-fax` · `sheet-composite` (several panels on one sheet, only some of them the plan — legends, schedules, photographs)

**B2. Not a plan at all**
`render-3d` — a 3D isometric or perspective view. Kept separate from every other tag because it is not a degraded plan that better handling could rescue: there is no orthographic projection, so no single scale describes it and no calibration is meaningful. **Refusing these is correct behaviour, not a gap.**

**C. Annotation load** — everything the filters must reject
`anno-none` · `anno-dimensions` · `anno-grid` · `anno-titleblock` · `anno-furniture` · `anno-hatchfill` · `anno-text-heavy` · `anno-watermark` · `anno-northarrow-scalebar`

**D. Geometry**
`geom-orthogonal` · `geom-angled` (any wall off-axis — **known unsupported**, tracked to size the gap) · `geom-curved` (**known unsupported**) · `geom-lshape` · `geom-courtyard` (a hole in the plan) · `geom-multi-unit` (several plans on one sheet — **known unsupported**: there is no crop step, so both plans trace into one wall network with no storey relationship. STATE.md finding 36)

**E. Dimensioning** — drives the calibration ladder, not the detector
`dim-imperial` · `dim-metric` · `dim-mixed` · `dim-none` · `dim-scalebar-only` · `dim-indic-numerals`

**F. Provenance**
`src-cad-export` · `src-scan-300dpi` · `src-scan-72dpi` · `src-photo` · `src-screenshot` · `src-listing-jpeg`

### Ground truth per drawing

Traced **by hand in the app**, exported as `.json`, and stored beside the image. Recorded alongside:

- the calibration used, and how it was obtained (which rung of the authority ladder);
- source resolution and the real-world dimensions of the building;
- who traced it and when — a hand trace is itself fallible, and disputes need an owner.

### Metrics — per tag, per drawing

| Metric | Definition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall recall** | matched ground-truth walls ÷ total | The headline. Misses cost the user manual tracing. |
| **Wall precision** | matched detections ÷ total detections | Spurious walls are worse than misses — the user must find and delete each one. |
| **Centreline error** | median \|detected − true\|, in mm at true scale | Feeds every area and therefore every rupee. |
| **Thickness error** | median, in mm | Feeds the BOQ once quantities exist. |
| **Fragmentation** | detections per ground-truth wall | >1 means healing failed; a chain of fragments is unusable. |
| **Fusion** | ground-truth walls per detection | >1 is the `mergeWallFaces` bug class. |
| **Runtime** | ms at source resolution | Budget: off the main thread, cancellable, with progress. |

### Acceptance, and how it is reported

There is no single pass mark. The output is a **table by tag**, published with each release, with a per-tag floor:

| Tag group | Recall floor | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| `wall-solid` + `sheet-white` + `anno-none` | 95% | Anything less is a bug in the easy case. |
| `wall-solid` + any annotation | 90% | The filters are the whole job. |
| `wall-outlined` / `wall-hatched` | 80% | Hard, common, and the reason `mergeWallFaces` exists. |
| `sheet-scan` / `sheet-photo` | 70% | Degraded input; the user expects to fix some. |
| `geom-angled` / `geom-curved` | **0% — expected** | Unsupported by construction. Tracked so the gap is sized, not hidden. |

**Silent truncation is forbidden.** If the corpus runner samples, caps, or skips, it says so in the report. A number that looks like full coverage and is not is worse than a lower number that is honest.

### What the app must say to the user

The corpus is also a UI specification. When detection is weak for a drawing's tags, the panel should say which kind of drawing this is and what to expect — not report a generic "no walls found". The existing message already tries this and is now partly stale: it says *"hatched, outlined, or angled ones are invisible to it"*, but `mergeWallFaces` handles outlined walls specifically.

## Running it

Not yet built. The runner belongs at `src/blueprint/corpus.test.ts`, gated behind an env flag so it does not slow the unit suite, reading from a `corpus/` directory that is **not committed** — real client drawings are confidential. It should emit the per-tag table as a build artefact.

The decoding seam already exists: `detectWallSegments` takes `RasterLike`, not `ImageData`, *"so the detector runs outside a browser too"*. `detectWalls.golden.test.ts` uses it with `pngjs`, and the corpus runner can use exactly the same path.
